Building with AI: field notes

Sujeet Mathew Jose · Berlin

Living document, expanded as I go

Notes to myself on working with AI as a product person who also builds. Less theory, more what actually holds up in daily use. Each entry starts as a stub and grows.

Concept

The harness

The scaffolding around a model that turns a raw capability into a reliable tool: the loop, the tools it can call, the context you feed it, the checks on its output. The model is the engine; the harness is the car.

To expand: why the harness matters more than the base model for most product work; what a good eval-in-the-loop harness looks like; where mine still leaks.
Concept

The LLM wiki

A durable, queryable memory that sits outside any single session, so the assistant starts warm instead of cold. The difference between explaining yourself every time and being understood by default.

To expand: how I structure mine; what earns a place in it vs. what stays ephemeral; the retrieval problem when it gets big.
Concept

Compacting

Deliberately compressing a long working context down to what still matters, so the model keeps its footing as a task runs long. Knowing what to throw away is the skill.

To expand: when to compact vs. restart; what always survives a compaction; the failure mode where you compact away the one detail you needed.
Concept

Context engineering

The discipline of deciding what goes into the model's window, in what order, at what fidelity. Prompt engineering is the sentence; context engineering is everything around it.

To expand: the budget mindset; retrieval vs. stuffing; why more context often makes output worse.
