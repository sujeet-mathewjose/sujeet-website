Zalando · product lead · 0→1

Beauty Advisor & Assurance

A personalised skincare matching programme in two modes. Assurance gives a product-page match verdict against skin type and concerns with a provable rationale; Advisor runs a guided diagnostic that ranks products from the full assortment in under four seconds. Both feed a Beauty Profile that compounds across sessions.

Live iOS DE, May 202628% perfect-match → cart€5.7–9M GMV modelled '26

What I built

Two modes on one engine. Assurance sits on the product page: a short quiz returns a match / no-match verdict against the customer's skin type and concerns, with a provable rationale or an alternative recommendation. Advisor sits on the category page: a guided diagnostic that shortlists ranked products from across the full assortment with science-backed rationale in under four seconds. Both feed a Beauty Profile that compounds across sessions.

Why it's hard

Suitability isn't a single score. The ranking formula combines skin-type fit, root-mean-square concern efficacy across seven dimensions, ingredient safety flags, and review signals aggregated across Zalando, Sephora, Amazon, and Douglas, pre-computed offline to hit sub-four-second latency. The harder constraint is correctness: a plausible-but-wrong recommendation in a skin-health category erodes trust faster than silence. Every output is validated against a dermatologist-approved framework and an automated eval engine. The rule engine is deterministic by design, not a generic LLM call, so every rationale Zalando shows is legally provable.

Commercial impact

Live on iOS DE, May 2026. 28% of customers receiving a perfect match added to cart. Revenue modelled at €5.7–9M incremental GMV in 2026 (skincare, 10% reach), scaling to €16–20M in 2027 with full category expansion. Part of a strategy targeting an increase in beauty order frequency from 1.8x to 2.5x per customer.

Zalando · shipped · 4 milestones

Beauty PDP

A full rebuild of the Zalando beauty product page, opening with AI-generated product attributes (skin benefits, skin sensitivity, the top notes of a perfume), plus enriched descriptions outside the accordion, a "How to Apply" section, the Beauty Set feature, a visual 40+ shade colour selector, and a curated storytelling rich content section.

~5% PDP conversion liftdouble-digit brand revenue growth '24–'25+8% add-to-wishlist conversion

What I built

A four-milestone rebuild of the beauty PDP. First position: AI-generated product attributes, skin benefits, skin sensitivity, or the top notes of a perfume, produced at a catalogue scale manual content production could never reach. Also delivered: product benefits and enriched descriptions outside the accordion; a "How to Apply" section absent from nearly every Zalando PDP; the Beauty Set feature for routine-based complementary discovery; a visual 40+ shade colour selector replacing a size dropdown; and a curated storytelling rich content section for luxury brands.

Why it's hard

374M PDP visits in 2023, conversion at 2.7% against a 3.4% industry average. The gap wasn't one missing feature, it was a structural mismatch between a fashion-built page and beauty's evaluation requirements. The work required coordinating product data quality, content production, brand development, legal, and three platform engineering teams at once, with a mid-programme pivot when data-quality timelines slipped and a prioritisation process across 26 candidate initiatives, all without stopping a live surface receiving hundreds of millions of visits.

Commercial impact

~5% PDP conversion improvement across 14 markets. Curated brand content: double-digit brand revenue growth across 2024 and 2025 combined, live across five luxury brands. Beauty Set: 30% click rate among viewers, +8% add-to-wishlist conversion, 0.3% haircare conversion lift confirmed. Colour picker: ~€1M incremental GMV expected. L'Oréal moved from vocal critic to vocal advocate, resulting in additional assortment and improved commercial terms.

Blibli · shipped · ML

ML-driven flash sales

A machine-learning rebuild of Blibli's flash sale engine, automating product selection, discount depth, and scheduling across a 1M+ SKU catalogue for 300M+ customers, replacing manual merchandising curation.

+5% overall revenue300M+ customers1M+ SKUs

What I built

An ML-driven revamp of the flash sale engine: selection of which products go on sale, how deep the discount runs, and when each slot schedules, plus the surrounding campaign operations to run it at marketplace scale.

Why it's hard

A flash sale is a multi-objective optimisation, margin against sell-through against traffic, run inside tight time windows across a huge catalogue. Manual curation couldn't react fast enough or price the right items deep enough, and every wrong call is money left on the table at 300M-customer scale.

Commercial impact

Roughly 5% overall company revenue lift. My first shipped ML product, and the start of a personalization thread that runs through everything since.

LLM Wiki, a second brain

A knowledge graph where every project, decision, and concept becomes a linked markdown note an LLM can read and traverse. The graph above is the real thing: the complete, continuously updated context of one live programme, from customer problem to commercial case to vision roadmap.

linked markdown notesLLM-readableone live programme, fully mapped

What it is

Interlinked notes covering the customer problem, commercial case, GTM plan, scoring pipeline, performance metrics, and vision roadmap of what I'm building. New notes link to existing ones, so context compounds instead of scattering across documents and chats.

Why it earns its place

An LLM with the wiki in context answers like a colleague who was in the room, not a stranger reading a brief. Cold-start explanation time drops to zero, and the wiki doubles as a single source of truth for humans too.

Where it's going

Today it holds the complete, updated detail of one project. We're now working with the tech team to convert it into a larger operating system for the entire cross-functional team.
